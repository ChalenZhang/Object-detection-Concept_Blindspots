# Data, Models, Statistics, and Illustration

The checkpoints and statistics are provided for non-commercial research. They do not grant rights to the source datasets beyond the original providers' terms. Dataset sources and original publications are listed in the [README](README.md#datasets).

The [Cityscapes license](https://www.cityscapes-dataset.com/license/) allows distribution of trained models and abstract derivatives that do not permit recovery of the dataset or something similar in character, and prohibits commercial use and redistribution of dataset images or modified datasets. The model and statistics packages contain learned weights and numerical research outputs, not Cityscapes image archives, segmentation masks, or original bounding-box annotation files. Use the original provider's authorized download process for Cityscapes images and annotations. See the [Cityscapes guide](Cityscapes/README.md#license-and-availability) for the specific clauses and the style-bank release status.

The separate RealDriveSim 6,000-image release contains an extracted subset of [RealDriveSim](https://realdrivesim.github.io/), credited to its original authors and redistributed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This dataset is not subject to the non-commercial restriction on our checkpoints and statistics. See its [attribution and processing notice](data/realdrivesim/README.md#source-and-license). It contains no Cityscapes images and no style-transferred images.

The single composite in `assets/styles-overview.jpg` is an attributed research illustration of appearance transformations. It is not covered by the software license and does not license reuse of the underlying images. Rights concerns can be reported through the repository's Issues page for prompt review and removal.
